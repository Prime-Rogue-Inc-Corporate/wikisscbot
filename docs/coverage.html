Hockey-simulation leagues

One piece of league software, STHS (SimonT Hockey Simulator), prints the IP address of each general manager who saves a lineup through its web client into a public transaction log. Those logs are the only fantasy-sport source found that publishes member IPs. 86 leagues answered; together they hold 27,460 lineup submissions this season, 147 of them from government ranges, in 16 leagues. The log timestamp is when the commissioner ran the simulation, not when the manager submitted, so it says nothing about time of day.

What this list is for

Absence of a result means nothing unless the place was actually checkable and actually answered. This page is the denominator. It is regenerated with the data; sites move between sections as they come and go.

An edit from a government network is not evidence of who made it, or that it was made in an official capacity. Most addresses are shared gateways used by hundreds or thousands of people. Team names are the leagues' own; managers are not identified.
