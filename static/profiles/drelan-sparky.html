One editing lineage across two government exits. DRELAN is the Department of National Defence's own gateway pool, four addresses until 2021 and two after that. SPARKY is the Shared Services Canada pair that took over its pages in November 2023 and kept editing the same regimental pages until Wikipedia stopped publishing IP addresses.

This profile describes network exit points, not people. A gateway can carry traffic for thousands of employees. An edit from one is not evidence of who made it, or that it was made in an official capacity. All counts come from this archive: every Wikimedia wiki, 1 January 2010 to 3 November 2025. Edits before 2010 are not counted.

The codename

DRELAN comes from the old block's registry name, DRE-LAN3. SPARKY is long-standing Canadian Armed Forces slang for a signaller. That fits because the new gateway's first recorded edits were the Royal Canadian Corps of Signals, a run of NATO radio-band pages and 21 Electronic Warfare Regiment.

Registration

  • DRELAN: 131.137.0.0/16, registry name DRE-LAN3, assigned to the Department of National Defence (101 Colonel By Drive) since November 1996, with DND technical contacts.
  • SPARKY: 205.192.0.0/14, registry name GTIS-CBLK, allocated since 1995 to what is now Shared Services Canada and announced by SSC's network (AS2675). Neither address has public reverse DNS.
  • Community labels: Wikipedia volunteers label DRELAN's addresses as DND. One label reads "Department of National Defence (Canada) via Shared Services Canada – Toronto". SPARKY is labelled only as Shared Services Canada.

  • One exit, four addresses. Traffic was spread across 131.137.245.206–.209, and the same page was often edited from one address and then another within the hour: about 100 such handoffs a year through 2015. .209 joined at the end of 2011.
  • A load-balancing change you can date from outside. Handoffs stopped in 2016: none across 586 edits. They returned briefly in early 2017, then all but vanished. The four addresses kept sharing pages, but each sitting now stayed on one address.
  • Four became two in October 2021. .208 and .209 last edited on 20 October 2021. .206 and .207 carried on alone.
  • The pool was DND's editing. From 2019 to 2023 it made 94–97% of all edits from 131.137.0.0/16 each year (for example, 509 of 543 in 2019 and 222 of 229 in 2023).
  • A pandemic dip. 509 edits in 2019, 200 in 2020. That's what an office network looks like when the office empties.

  • SPARKY is itself a pool. Like DRELAN before 2016, it hands the same page between .44 and .76 within the hour: 10 times in 2024 and 36 in 2025. On 21 October 2025 the Commander of the Royal Canadian Air Force page got 13 edits in one day, 7 from .44 and 6 from .76.

Same pages, both eras

88 of the 310 pages SPARKY edited (28%) had been edited earlier by DRELAN. For 19 other government address blocks active over the same period, it was 28 of 1,499 pages (1.9%). The highest single block was 4 of 24. Examples, with DRELAN's last edit and SPARKY's first:

  • 4th Canadian Division Support Group: 13 edits, last May 2022 → Feb 2025

Context

The National Security and Intelligence Committee of Parliamentarians reported in February 2022 that every Shared Services Canada core partner had moved onto SSC's Enterprise Internet Service except National Defence, which was due to move in 2021–22. The editing record puts the move at roughly November 2023 to April 2024. We found no public SSC document confirming the date.

After November 2025

On 4 November 2025 Wikipedia stopped showing IP addresses for logged-out edits, so SPARKY's later edits aren't public and this archive doesn't collect them. Wikipedia's public block log still records actions against the addresses themselves:

  • 23 August 2026, 04:58 UTC: 205.193.239.76 was blocked from editing one page, "List of federal political parties in Canada", for one month (disruptive editing). In the days before, temporary accounts had repeatedly changed the Liberal Party's listed position on that page from "centre to centre-left" to "centre-left to left-wing". One of those accounts was later blocked for block evasion.
  • 23 August 2026, 22:55 UTC: both 205.193.239.44 and .76 were blocked from all logged-out editing for three months, through late November 2026. The page was protected the same day.

The block log names the addresses, not the accounts behind any edit. The sequence above comes from public logs only, and no attempt has been made to reveal temporary-account IPs.

Confidence

High that SPARKY replaced DRELAN as the main Wikipedia-visible internet exit for National Defence users between November 2023 and April 2024. This is an inference from public records, not an official confirmation, and SPARKY remains a shared SSC gateway.
