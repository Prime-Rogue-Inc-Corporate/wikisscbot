What this list is for

Absence of a result means nothing unless the place was actually checkable and actually answered. This page is the denominator. It is regenerated with the data; sites move between sections as they come and go.

This page covers wikis only. Other kinds of site that publish visitor addresses are tracked in a separate Prime Rogue project.

An edit from a government network is not evidence of who made it, or that it was made in an official capacity. Most addresses are shared gateways used by hundreds or thousands of people.
