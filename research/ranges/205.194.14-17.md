# 205.194.14.0/24 to 205.194.17.0/24

Checked 2026-11-12 against the archive (2010 to 3 November 2025). Raised by the league-log survey, which saw 205.194.15.x and 205.194.16.x addresses next to 205.194.16.3.

## Result
These four /24s are Shared Services Canada address space that came back into use in 2021–2022 as separately announced blocks. Wikipedia edits from them start in April 2024. The department or departments behind them are not known. The archive does not show that the addresses form one exit pool.

- **The other addresses** make a few edits each on unrelated general topics, in English and French.
- **Host numbers repeat across blocks:** .37 to .40 appear in all four blocks, and make up nearly every editing address outside 205.194.16.3. That pattern is what four similarly configured gateway pools would look like, but it is an observation about numbering, not evidence of a link.
- **No shared pages:** no page in the archive was edited by two different addresses from 205.194.15.x or 205.194.16.x. The test that showed 205.193.239.44 and .76 to be one pool (the same page handed back and forth within minutes) finds nothing here.
