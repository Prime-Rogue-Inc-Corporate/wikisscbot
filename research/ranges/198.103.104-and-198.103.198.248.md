# 198.103.104.11 and 198.103.198.248/29: the foreign-service gateways

Checked 2026-11-13 against the archive (2008 to 3 November 2025). The reverse DNS, registry and routing work is the Gateway profiles chat's (`osint-gateway-lineages-round2`); this note records what the labels do with it.

## 198.103.104.11: labelled Foreign Affairs from 2011

- **Evidence:** reverse DNS `gate1.dfait-maeci.gc.ca`, forward-confirmed (looked up 2026-11-13); ARIN registers 198.103.104.0/24 as `DFAIT-MAECI`. The address name and the registrant are two separate records.
- **Behaviour agrees:** 128 of its 132 edits to Canadian embassy, consulate, ambassador-list and Trade Commissioner Service pages in 2011–2019 came from it.
- **Archive:** 1,469 edits. 236 in 2008, none in 2009 or 2010, then every year from 2011 to its last edit on 15 October 2020.
- **The 2008 edits are not labelled Foreign Affairs.** None of them is to a foreign-service page (they are on genealogy, Ottawa, rail and similar topics), and the address was silent for two years before the foreign-service run began. The reverse DNS is today's record, not a 2008 one. So `config/ranges_confirmed.json` starts the label on 2011-01-01 and ends it on 2020-10-15. Before and after, the address keeps the generic "Shared Services Canada" label.
- **Label:** "Department of Foreign Affairs and International Trade (now Global Affairs Canada)". 1,233 edits take it.

## 198.103.198.248/29: stays "Shared Services Canada"

- Eight addresses (mainly .250, .249, .248), 311 edits, from 29 October 2020, fourteen days after 198.103.104.11 stopped.
- They took over the same kind of pages (29 of 33 diplomatic-mission edits in 2021–2025) and carry over pages from gate1 at 7.4%, against 0.5% for control blocks.
- That is behaviour, not a record. The registry entry (`GTIS-C1-198`) is Shared Services Canada's and there is no reverse DNS naming a department. The label is left alone; "likely Global Affairs Canada by behaviour" belongs in the profile, not in the range files.
