# Range notes

Short, dated notes on single addresses and small ranges: what the archive shows, how the range is labelled and routed, and what is not established. They are working notes for attribution at the level of a network or institution. Nothing here identifies a person.

Figures come from the archive on `main` (2010 or 2008 to 3 November 2025) on the date in each note. Routing dates come from `config/ranges_ssc_bgp.json` (RIPEstat). Registry statements are marked as such; an old registry name is "registered to", not proof of who uses the range today.
