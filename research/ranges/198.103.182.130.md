# 198.103.182.130: lead, Commissioner of Official Languages

Checked 2026-11-13 against the archive (2008 to 3 November 2025). Raised by the Gateway 184 chat.

## Result
A strong lead, not a confirmation. The reverse DNS names the Office of the Commissioner of Official Languages, and the address's edits are concentrated on that office and its commissioners to a degree no other address comes near. It is **not** added to `config/ranges_confirmed.json`: the reverse record has no matching forward record, so it is one record plus behaviour, and the Coordinator's rule for 198.103.109.141 (a second independent source) applies.

## Records
- Reverse DNS: `rvp.clo-ocol.gc.ca` and `vpn.ocol-clo.gc.ca` (looked up by the Gateway 184 chat on 2026-11-12). `clo-ocol` is the office's domain.
- The forward lookup of those names did not resolve to this address. A reverse record can be stale or set by whoever controls the reverse zone, so on its own it is weaker than a forward-confirmed name.
- It is the only address in 198.103.182.0/24 with edits in the archive.

## What the archive shows
- **270 edits**, 26 March 2009 to 31 December 2019: English Wikipedia 153, French Wikipedia 111, Wikidata 6.
- **230 of them in 2018**, nearly all between January and April.
- **86 edits are on official-languages pages.** Every commissioner of official languages since the 1980s has a page edited from it: Graham Fraser (20, plus 4 on the French page), Raymond Théberge (14), Dyane Adam (14), Victor Goldbloom (12), D'Iberville Fortier (6). Also the Official Languages Act (13), the office's own page in English and French (10), and related pages.
- **43 of the 48 archive edits** to the pages for the Commissioner of Official Languages, its office, Graham Fraser and the Official Languages Act came from this one address.
- No weekend edits.
- In 2018 the same address also built up Graham Fraser's biography by more than 12 KB (reported in the 2018 summary).

## Second source that would settle it
Any one of: a forward-confirmed name for the address; a registry record naming the office; the office's own published network details; a historical DNS record.
